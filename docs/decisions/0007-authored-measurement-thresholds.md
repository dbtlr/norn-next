# 0007 — measurement thresholds are authored constraints

Calibration envelopes and runtime thresholds are authored constraints changed only through reviewed edits with stated grounds. Deriving them automatically from previous observations would ratify drift and depend on history that may disappear. Measurements may justify an edit, but private collections, previous runs, and retained artifacts never redefine acceptability themselves.
