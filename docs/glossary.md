# Glossary

The domain terms of `norn`, and what each one means here specifically.

The glossary is empty on purpose. **Terms enter with the layers that earn them** — a term
is added when a landed contract depends on the distinction it draws, not in advance of one.
Until then, [`architecture.md`](architecture.md) carries the vocabulary in context, and
[`decisions/`](decisions/README.md) will as each ADR lands.
